About CampusNest SL

Our Story & Mission

Students who move to Freetown to study often find it difficult to locate safe, affordable, and genuine accommodation near their campus. Rooms are traditionally advertised through scattered informal channels where prices and conditions are unclear.

Our Mission: To design a clear, trustworthy, and mobile-friendly platform that reduces the time, cost, and risk involved in finding student accommodation in Freetown. We place trust, price clarity, and ease of direct contact first.
